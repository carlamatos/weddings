About the show

Chrome, curves & classics

Fifty years of chrome, curves and horsepower. Collectors and enthusiasts from across the valley roll in with everything from pre-war roadsters to 1970s muscle. Stroll the lot, talk shop with the owners, and stay for live music, food trucks and the awards.
